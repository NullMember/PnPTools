PnPTools

A toolbox for print-and-play. Runs in your browser, works offline.

Which tool do I need?

  • Cards are inside a PDF of sheets → CardCrop
  • Tokens or cards on a scan → Freeform crop
  • Scanned or photographed cards look uneven → Align
  • Card images have no bleed → Bleed
  • Coins, tokens, tiles of mixed shapes → Layout
  • Print a rulebook or card sheets → Booklet
  • You use a Cricut like cutting machine → Cut
  • The deck needs a box → TuckBox

1 Get the images from a PnP file

CardCrop

Crop cards out of a printable PDF sheet (front/back matching for duplex and fold layouts), or cut any shape out of scans with freeform crop — detected automatically, exported as transparent PNGs.

Open tool →

2 Clean them up optional

Align

Batch colour, rotation, scale and position alignment for scanned or photographed cards, with crop/fill stabilization.

Open tool →

Bleed

Add printer bleed to card images by extending, mirroring or filling their edges, with white-border detection and edge trimming.

Open tool →

3 Lay out for printing

Layout

Pack mixed-shape pieces — coins, tokens, hex tiles — tightly onto as few sheets as possible using their transparent outlines, with outline bleed, cut lines, backs and an SVG cut file.

Open tool →

Booklet

Tile images and PDF pages into a grid across sheets, or impose them as a saddle-stitch booklet, with borders, spacing and page numbers.

Open tool →

4 Cut for cutting machines

Cut

Generate a print-then-cut grid, draw cut/score/emboss line art on a card, assemble per-card designs into a full sheet, and calibrate cut registration.

Open tool →

5 Box it

TuckBox

Printable tuck boxes, two-piece lid-and-base boxes and sleeves sized to your deck, with artwork on each panel, cut and fold lines, and an SVG cut file.

Open tool →
