# PnP Tuck Box

Client-side generator for card-deck packaging:

- **Classic tuck box** (tuck-in lid and bottom, thumb notch, glue flap)
- **Two-piece box** (base tray plus a slightly larger lid)
- **Sleeve / wrap** (open band)

Sized from the card size and deck thickness (cards × thickness per card,
sleeved or not) with clearance and paper thickness. Add artwork per panel and a
background colour, then download a print PDF (art plus cut and fold lines) or an
SVG with cut and score layers for print-then-cut machines. Everything runs in the
browser; nothing is uploaded.

Part of [PnPTools](https://github.com/NullMember/PnPTools).
